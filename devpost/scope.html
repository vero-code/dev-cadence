Dev Cadence

An in-context Chrome extension side panel that realistically paces hackathon workloads directly on Devpost, bridging the gap between registration and final submission.

The Unique Kernel

Instead of another detached to-do list, Dev Cadence couples directly with the Devpost browsing experience as a persistent side panel. It visually maps commitments against remaining open days in the month—giving developers immediate clarity on whether they can realistically deliver before hitting "Register".

“I’ve noticed a huge discrepancy between the number of hackathons participants register for and what they actually submit... great ideas never get presented due to poor management. The solution needs to work in the background, showing them how to plan directly on the Devpost page.”
